3. DEMOGRAPHIC CHARACTERISTICS BY RETENTION STATUS

   a) Indigenous Status (Strongest Predictor)
      - Contingency table with retention rates by ethnicity
      - Statistical test results (χ² = 109.72, p < 0.001)
      - Effect size: Cramér's V = 0.174 (moderate)
      - Interpretation: Indigenous 42.2% retained vs. Non-indigenous 25.6%
      - Implication: Retained sample overrepresents indigenous participants

   b) Gender (Moderate Predictor)
      - Contingency table by gender
      - Statistical test results (χ² = 35.10, p < 0.001)
      - Effect size: Cramér's V = 0.099
      - Retention: Female 37.5% vs. Male 27.7%

   c) Age (Small-to-Moderate Predictor)
      - Descriptive statistics by retention status
      - Statistical test results (t = -3.72, p < 0.001)
      - Effect size: Cohen's d = -0.130
      - Retained mean age: 46.6 years vs. Lost: 48.8 years

   d) Education Level (No Significant Difference)
      - Contingency table across education levels
      - Statistical test: χ² = 8.51, p = 0.579 (ns)
      - Conclusion: Education NOT a source of attrition bias

4. PSYCHOLOGICAL AND BEHAVIORAL VARIABLES

   a) Outgroup Trust - PRIMARY OUTCOME (No Bias - Excellent for Validity!)
      - Descriptive statistics by retention status
      - Statistical test: t = -1.51, p = 0.130 (ns)
      - Effect size: Cohen's d = -0.054 (negligible)
      - Interpretation: No significant difference—unbiased baseline

   b) Intergroup Contact Frequency (Significant Predictor)
      - Descriptive statistics by retention status
      - Statistical test: t = 4.10, p < 0.001
      - Effect size: Cohen's d = 0.145 (small)
      - Retained: M = 3.61 vs. Lost: M = 3.42
      - Interpretation: Higher frequency predicts retention (selection bias)

   c) Contact Quantity - Number of Acquaintances (Marginal)
      - Descriptive statistics by retention status
      - Statistical test: t = 1.71, p = 0.087 (marginal, ns)
      - Effect size: Cohen's d = 0.062 (negligible)
